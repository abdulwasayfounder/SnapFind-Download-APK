Feature Architecture

Everything Built Inside SnapFind AI

Designed specifically for Android to transform messy camera roll screenshots into an organized, instant-access visual database.

1. AI Screenshot Search

Searches through the semantic meaning of your captures. You don't have to recall specific filenames or dates—describe the item conceptually and SnapFind retrieves the relevant image.

2. OCR Text Search

Extracts and indexes text from images. Search for specific words, tracking numbers, flight codes, dollar totals, or street addresses shown on screen.

3. Smart Snaps

Automatic categorical classification. Automatically identifies whether an image belongs to Banking, Receipts, Work, Travel, Shopping, or the Private Vault.

4. Banking & Payments

Dedicated filtering for payment proofs, Zelle/Venmo confirmations, wire receipts, and invoice summaries with automatic currency and total balance detection.

5. Sensitive Protection

Detects sensitive content such as ID cards, medical lab results, and confidential documents, proposing isolation and safeguarding them from accidental exposure.

6. Collection Lock

Locks any individual Smart Snap collection behind your Android lock screen security (fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN code).

7. Secure Vault

An isolated encrypted container inside the app. Vault screenshots are excluded from general photo pickers, thumbnails, and quick system search widgets.

8. Duplicate Prevention

Compares image perceptual hashes in real-time. Identical screenshots taken in bursts are filtered out, ensuring zero wasted memory and preventing search clutter.

9. Offline Indexing

Local screenshot detection and cached search indexes function continuously without an internet connection. Vault access is completely offline.

10. Website & URL Detection

Parses visible links, hostnames, and web addresses inside the screenshot, enabling 1-tap copy or instant redirection to the webpage in your default browser.

11. QR Code Detection

Decodes QR codes captured in photos and screenshots. Reads Wi-Fi network credentials, URLs, ticket validation codes, and digital contact cards.

12. Fast Screenshot Indexing

Lightweight Android background service catches new captures within seconds of the shutter trigger, updating indexes without draining your battery.

13. Natural Language Search

Understands queries like “my python code snippet”, “screenshot with hotel reservation”, or “electricity bill paid yesterday”.

Web & Barcode Intelligence

Direct Link & QR Extraction

Screenshots frequently contain web links and QR codes that are tedious to type out manually. SnapFind inspects the image pixels directly:

  • URL & Domain Detection: Identifies standard protocols (https://), top-level domains, and shortlinks.
  • 1-Tap Action: Quickly copy detected URLs to clipboard or launch them safely in your preferred browser.
  • Legible QR Decoding: Reads standard QR patterns present in captured boarding passes, event badges, and Wi-Fi shares.

Storage Efficiency

Zero Redundant Processing

Accidental double-presses and duplicate screenshots clutter galleries and waste memory. SnapFind prevents duplicates at the source:

  • Instant Hash Detection: Identifies matching pixel hashes before triggering heavy OCR computation.
  • Quota & Storage Protection: Prevents repetitive indexing from depleting background processing quotas or device memory.
  • Clean Search Results: Ensures you only see one canonical version of a screenshot in your search list.
