Privacy Policy

Transparent, straightforward disclosure on how SnapFind handles screenshot media, OCR text, AI processing, and security.

Core Privacy Principle: SnapFind AI treats your screenshots as sensitive personal assets. We do not sell your data, do not run ad networks against your captures, and allow you to permanently delete any indexed screenshot at any time.

1. What Information SnapFind Processes

When you use SnapFind AI on Android or via the companion web client, the application processes the following categories of data:

  • Screenshot Media: Image files captured or selected by you from your device gallery.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Tokens: Text extracted from legible areas of your screenshots (such as order numbers, confirmation codes, addresses, and flight details).
  • Visual & Categorical Metadata: Image dimensions, creation timestamps, perceptual hashes (to prevent duplicates), and category assignments (such as Banking, Receipts, Work, Travel).
  • Search Queries: Natural-language queries you enter into the search bar to locate specific captures.

2. How Screenshots Are Processed

Screenshot processing operates under a clear separation between local device indexing and cloud-dependent operations:

  • Local On-Device Detection: The Android app detects when a new screenshot file is added to your device storage. It calculates a lightweight perceptual hash to identify duplicates before performing heavier operations.
  • Text & OCR Indexing: Image text is scanned and converted into indexable searchable tokens.
  • AI Understanding: When connected, deep semantic modeling categorizes the screenshot into appropriate Smart Snaps and enables natural conversational queries. When offline, deep semantic requests pause in a secure local queue until internet access is restored.

3. Private Content, Locking & The Vault

SnapFind AI includes dedicated controls for sensitive materials such as medical records, banking statements, and government IDs:

  • Screenshots marked as Private or assigned to the Vault are excluded from general search results and gallery carousels.
  • Access to Vault items requires device-level biometric authentication (fingerprint, facial recognition) or your device PIN code.
  • Vault biometric checks are handled locally via Android BiometricPrompt APIs and are never transmitted over external networks.

4. Data Storage & User Ownership

Your screenshots remain strictly user-specific:

  • No user can access, query, or browse screenshots belonging to another user.
  • Local cache files remain within the Android application's protected sandbox directory.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

You maintain total control over screenshot retention:

  • When you delete a screenshot within the application, its extracted OCR text, categorical labels, search index tokens, and image references are permanently deleted.
  • You may request complete account data wiping or remove the Android application to instantly clear local cached data.

6. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To deliver backend synchronization and deep AI capabilities, SnapFind relies on trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Cloud Hosting & Compute: Encrypted backend endpoints provide database persistence and API mediation.
  • AI Inference APIs: High-parameter language models evaluate OCR tokens and visual metadata to return natural-language matches. Media tokens processed for inference are governed by standard API privacy protections and are not used to train generic third-party advertising models.

7. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical measures:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption for all network traffic in transit.
  • Strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users only query their own data.
  • No hardcoded API credentials or service-role keys in distributed client code.
