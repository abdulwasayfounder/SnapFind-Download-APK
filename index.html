User-Centric Privacy Philosophy

Your Visual Memory Belongs to You

Screenshots capture intimate fragments of your life: banking statements, conversations, medical appointments, and passwords. SnapFind AI is engineered with rigorous data boundaries.

User-Specific Ownership

Your screenshots and OCR tokens are bound strictly to your authenticated instance. No other user can query or view your image library.

Lockable Collections & Vault

Mark sensitive items with PIN or biometric lock. Locked items are excluded from unauthenticated quick previews and global search.

Permanent Deletion Control

When you delete a screenshot in SnapFind, its indexed tokens, OCR text, and metadata are permanently expunged.

Zero Ad-Data Sharing

Website advertising scripts are strictly isolated: no screenshot media, OCR text, or metadata is ever shared with advertising providers.

Engineering Transparency

Offline Indexing & AI Processing

Clear, honest engineering on how SnapFind operates offline versus connected.

When You Are Offline

  • Local Screenshot Detection: New captures saved on your Android device are detected immediately by local media observers.
  • Instant Local Indexing: Timestamps, perceptual duplicate hashes, and cached OCR records remain searchable locally.
  • Locked Vault Access: Biometric and PIN unlock operate entirely on-device without network requirements.

When Cloud AI is Needed

  • Semantic Reasoning: High-level natural-language reasoning queues safely until connectivity returns.
  • No Fake Offline Claims: We never falsely claim that multi-billion parameter cloud AI runs locally on mobile silicon.
  • Queue Sync: Once internet access is restored, queued items process automatically in the background.
